AgaveDex Watch — Data Deletion Instructions

繁體中文

Effective date: 2026-10-30
Last updated: 2026-10-31

App: AgaveDex Watch (Meta App ID 1093481716927882), operated by Agavedex

Contact: bo@forte.fyi

AgaveDex Watch is a read-only internal research tool. It reads public Threads profiles and public posts through the Threads API for competitor and market research. It has no user accounts and no login for the public, so there is no in-app "delete" button. To delete your data, follow the steps below.

These instructions cover the copies held by AgaveDex Watch. Your original Threads posts stay on Threads. To delete an Agavedex mobile app account, go to Account Settings → Delete Account in the mobile app. See the Agavedex mobile app privacy policy.

How to request deletion

  • Email bo@forte.fyi with the subject "AgaveDex Watch data deletion".
  • Include your Threads username (for example @yourname). If you only want specific posts removed, include their links.
  • We may ask you to confirm from your Threads account, so we know the request really comes from you.

What happens next

  • Within 30 days of receiving your request, we delete the raw Threads data we hold about that account or those posts: profile information, post text, links, and metrics.
  • Within the same 30 days, we also remove the matching usernames, post text, links and other identifying details from our internal notes and summaries.
  • We reply by email to confirm when deletion is complete.

Automatic deletion

  • Raw API data is deleted automatically no later than 12 months after collection. The same deadline applies to Threads data in our internal notes and summaries, including usernames, post text and links.
  • If a post or account is deleted or made private on Threads, we remove the related data, including references in our notes and summaries, when we become aware of it or at our next review.

Removing app access (for the authorising account)

Only the Agavedex team’s Threads account authorises Watch. The account holder can revoke access by removing AgaveDex Watch in the Threads settings for apps and websites. To have stored data deleted as well, email us as described above. Anyone whose public data Watch holds can request deletion without authorising the tool.

AgaveDex Watch — 資料刪除說明（繁體中文）

English

生效日期： 2026-10-30
最後更新： 2026-10-31

聯絡： bo@forte.fyi

AgaveDex Watch 係只讀嘅內部研究工具，透過 Threads API 讀取公開帳號同公開帖文，用嚟做競爭對手同市場研究。本工具冇公開用戶帳號，所以 app 入面冇刪除按鈕，請用以下方法申請刪除。

本說明只適用於 AgaveDex Watch 保存的副本。你的 Threads 原帖會保留在 Threads。如要刪除 Agavedex 手機應用程式帳戶，請在該應用程式選擇「帳戶設定」→「刪除帳戶」。詳見《Agavedex 手機應用程式私隱政策》。

點樣申請

  • 電郵到 bo@forte.fyi，標題寫 「AgaveDex Watch data deletion」。
  • 寫低你嘅 Threads 用戶名（例如 @yourname）。如果只想刪某幾篇帖文，請附上帖文連結。
  • 我哋可能會請你用 Threads 帳號確認，證明係你本人提出。

之後會點處理

  • 收到申請後 30 日內刪除我們持有的相關原始資料，包括個人檔案資料、帖文內容、連結及數據。
  • 我們亦會在同一期限內刪除內部筆記及摘要中的相關用戶名稱、帖文內容、連結及其他可識別資料。
  • 完成之後會用電郵通知你。

自動刪除

原始 API 資料自收集日起最多保留 12 個月，之後會自動刪除。內部筆記及摘要中的 Threads 資料，包括用戶名稱、帖文內容及連結，均受同一期限約束。如果帖文或帳號在 Threads 上被刪除或設為私人，我們會在得悉後或下次檢查時移除相關資料，包括筆記及摘要中的引用。

撤銷應用程式授權（供授權帳號使用）

只有 Agavedex 團隊的 Threads 帳號會授權 Watch。帳號持有人可在 Threads 的應用程式及網站設定中移除 AgaveDex Watch，藉此撤銷授權。如需一併刪除已儲存的資料，請按上述步驟電郵申請。任何人的公開資料如被 Watch 收錄，均可申請刪除，無需授權本工具。
